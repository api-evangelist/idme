International Revenue Share Fraud (IRSF), also called SMS pumping or toll fraud, is both a cyberattack and a financial scam. Distinct from traditional threats, it requires no malware, credential theft, or successful login to succeed. Instead, it exploits international telecom pricing structures to siphon funds directly from organizations that utilize SMS-based OTP (one-time password).
